


\section{Expected contributions}

The following is list of deliverables which should result from the
work proposed in this research:

A unified framework to extract scene information.  Starting from the
bottom up with a module to find stationary sections.  Then a
collection of modules to extract the different bits of
information\ldots hopefully they are individually robust and accurate
but could potentially depend on other modules.

We expect to make contributions in several domains. 

Contributions to camera and sensor calibration:

\begin{description}

\item[Automatic camera geo-localization] A method for determining the
  camera location.

\item[Theoretical bounds of the localization algorithm] A statement
  about the conditions that are required for the localization
  procedure to obtain a given accuracy level.  This result will be
  applicable to other sensors and contexts.

\item[Automatic camera calibration] A method for camera calibration
  based on the dependence of images on natural variations in the
  scene.

\end{description}

Contributions to scene understanding:

\begin{description}

\item[Depth map generation] A method for extracting depth information
  from image sequences from static cameras.

\item[Scene motion segmentation] A method for characterizing motion
  present in a scene.

\end{description}
